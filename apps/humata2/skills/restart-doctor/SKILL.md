# Restart doctor

Read `guide.md` and follow its steps in order: read the restart report
before you change anything, work out the cause, apply the safe repairs, ask
before any repair that could lose something, and leave a diagnosis.

`shared/basics.md` says how `humata` answers and what its exit codes mean.
Both paths are relative to this skill's folder.
