# Frames: delete many sessions at once, or all of them

HTML mockups, not renders of the app. They are laid out at 1280x760, the size of the app's design frames, and shot at 1.25x, so each PNG is 1600x950. Sources are in `src/` (`sh src/shoot.sh <batch>` rebuilds and screenshots one batch). The sidebar, Home, the machine page, the palette, Settings and the sheet copy the approved design frames (16, 25, 59, 30, 29, 71, 55, 65).

## Batch 1: pick sessions, then the plan
- `m1-1-sidebar-select.png`: I ⌘-click a session in the sidebar, then tick two more. I see a tick box on every session, channel and machine, my three ticked rows tinted, one fork ticked for me in a paler tick because it shares its parent's folder, and a card at the foot of the sidebar: "4 selected", Cancel, "Delete 4 sessions…". Shows: the count on the button is what will be deleted; a channel or machine box picks all of its sessions; an offline machine and an empty channel can't be ticked.
- `m1-2-plan-picked.png`: I press "Delete 4 sessions…". I see one sheet: three lines of totals on top (terminals that end, sessions with unpushed work, forks), then every session by channel with its agent, machine and live or archived, the terminals it ends, its unpushed work with a "Delete that work too" box, the fork that goes with its parent and the two forks that stay. Shows: the Delete button is off until the unticked box for Refund retries is ticked, and the line beside Cancel says so.
- `m1-3-plan-all-empty.png`: I choose "Delete all sessions…". I see the same sheet for all 23 sessions: the totals, the list scrolling inside the sheet with quiet sessions folded into one line per channel, the machine that can't be reached named in the totals, the Desk line, and a field to type 23. Shows: nothing typed, two work boxes unticked, so the red button is off and the hint names both.
- `m1-4-plan-stale.png`: I press Delete, but a commit and a terminal arrived after the plan was made. I see the sheet again with an amber notice on top: nothing was deleted, what changed, and that this is the new plan. The two changed sessions carry a "changed" tag and the new items are marked "(new)". Shows: the box on the session whose work changed is cleared and must be ticked again; the other box keeps its tick.

## Batch 2: the other lists, the entries, and after
- `m2-1-home-select-channel.png`: On Home I press Select, then pick "# payments-rewrite" from the Select menu in the bar. I see tick boxes on every row, the channel's five rows ticked, and a bar along the bottom: "6 selected", a note that one of them is not on Home, the keys, Cancel and "Delete 6 sessions…". Shows: the menu picks every session in a channel or on a machine; row buttons are hidden while selecting; Select in the toolbar reads Done.
- `m2-2-machine-select-all.png`: On devbox-1's page I press Select, then tick "Select all on devbox-1". I see all six sessions ticked and the same bar. Shows: the list heading becomes the select-all box with "6 of 6" and Done.
- `m2-3-palette.png`: I type "delete" in the palette. I see "Select sessions…" first and "Delete all sessions…" second, in red, with how many sessions and that it shows the plan first. Shows: Return never lands on Delete all by default, and it has no shortcut.
- `m2-4-settings-storage.png`: I open Settings > Storage. I see the machines, then a Sessions card with one row, "Delete all sessions", what it covers, and a red-outlined "Delete all sessions…" button; under it, how to delete only some. Shows: the entry lives with storage and trash, apart from everyday settings.
- `m2-5-plan-all-typed-dark.png`: In the delete-all sheet I tick the work boxes and type 23. I see the list scrolled to its end (the last channels and gpu-box, "can't reach, left alone", with its four sessions named), the field holding 23 and a filled red "Delete all 23 sessions". Shows: dark mode; the only filled red button in the app is this one, and only once the count matches.
- `m2-6-after-delete.png`: I confirm the delete of the four sessions. I see them gone from the sidebar and Home, the two forks that stayed moved up under the channel, and a toast: "Deleted 4 sessions. They are in the trash on This Mac and devbox-1 for 30 days." Shows: no Undo on the toast.
